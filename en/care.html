CARE · CLINICAL ASSESSMENT

The same symptom
can have different causes

We review when it began, how it recurs, and which symptoms accompany it
to determine the appropriate direction of care.

01 / FOCUS

Cognitive function and concentration

When sustained attention becomes difficult or mental fatigue develops,
we assess sleep quality, tension, and daily rhythm together.

Common concerns
Students preparing for exams, office workers, difficulty concentrating, brain fog, memory concerns
What DEAR considers
Sleep quality, accumulated fatigue, tension, and daily rhythm

02 / CALM

Tension response and sleep imbalance

When anxiety, hyperarousal, or difficulty initiating or maintaining sleep occurs,
we assess stress responses and autonomic nervous system patterns.

Common concerns
Anxiety, insomnia, autonomic imbalance, panic symptoms, tension
What DEAR considers
Stress response, sleep patterns, nervous system tension, and capacity for recovery

03 / RESTORE

Fatigue and reduced recovery

When fatigue and low energy persist despite rest,
we assess current stamina, digestion, and pace of recovery.

Common concerns
Chronic fatigue · Burnout · Low energy · Recovery care · Constitutional herbal medicine · Gongjindan
What DEAR considers
Current energy, pace of recovery, daily fatigue, constitution, and purpose for taking herbal medicine

04 / RELIEF

Pain and physical discomfort

For pain, headache, or digestive discomfort,
we assess symptom patterns, aggravating factors, and functional limitations.

Common concerns
Pain, headaches, migraines, indigestion, constipation, enteritis
What DEAR considers
Symptom patterns · Everyday aggravating factors · Tension and circulation · Gastrointestinal condition

05 / SHAPE

Weight and daily rhythm

When weight change, swelling, or reduced metabolism is present,
we review appetite, sleep, digestion, activity, and daily patterns.

Common concerns
Weight management, swelling, slower metabolism, poor circulation, body-shape concerns
What DEAR considers
Appetite, sleep, digestion, activity level, and everyday routines
