CARE

Five areas of care,
starting with how you feel today

It is okay if your symptoms are difficult to put into words.
At DEAR, we begin with the discomfort you feel and the changes in your daily life,
then consider your condition through five areas of care.

01 / FOCUS

Time to focus

When study, work, and fatigue leave your mind feeling foggy,
we look at what may be making it difficult to stay focused.

Common concerns
Students preparing for exams, office workers, difficulty concentrating, brain fog, memory concerns
What DEAR considers
Sleep quality, accumulated fatigue, tension, and daily rhythm

02 / CALM

When your mind cannot settle

When anxiety, tension, or sensitivity persists
and it becomes difficult to fall asleep or rest.

Common concerns
Anxiety, insomnia, autonomic imbalance, panic symptoms, tension
What DEAR considers
Stress response, sleep patterns, nervous system tension, and capacity for recovery

03 / RESTORE

When you need to restore your energy

When tiredness remains despite enough rest
and you feel that recovery is slower than before.

Common concerns
Chronic fatigue · Burnout · Low energy · Recovery care · Constitutional herbal medicine · Gongjindan
What DEAR considers
Current energy, pace of recovery, daily fatigue, constitution, and purpose for taking herbal medicine

04 / RELIEF

When discomfort interrupts your day

Like pain, headache, or digestive discomfort.
Let’s first look at the body signals that you are feeling right now.

Common concerns
Pain, headaches, migraines, indigestion, constipation, enteritis
What DEAR considers
Symptom patterns · Everyday aggravating factors · Tension and circulation · Gastrointestinal condition

05 / SHAPE

The beginning of healthy change

Beyond weight and body shape, we also consider metabolism, swelling,
and the daily habits connected to changes in your body.

Common concerns
Weight management, swelling, slower metabolism, poor circulation, body-shape concerns
What DEAR considers
Appetite, sleep, digestion, activity level, and everyday routines

BEGIN WITH YOUR STORY

Not sure where your concerns fit?

You do not need to describe everything perfectly.
Begin with what feels uncomfortable now
and what has changed in your daily life.
