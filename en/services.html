BE DEER Weight Care

02 / DEAR GONGJINDAN

Dear Gongjindan

What makes good Gongjindan
starts with the steps you can’t see.

DEAR doesn’t buy ready-made Gongjindan from outside suppliers.
We choose the herbs and compound it here at the clinic.
After looking at your current health and why you want to take it,
we explain the prescription and how to take it.

  1. 01Director Minji Kim compounds Gongjindan in-house.
  2. 02We manage every step, from choosing and storing herbs to compounding.
  3. 03We advise on when and how to take it, based on your health and goals.

03 / KOREAN HERBAL MEDICINE

In-house Constitutional Herbal Medicine

Prepared in-house
for each person’s current condition.

We handle it all in-house: receiving, storing and pre-processing herbs,
decocting herbal medicine and compounding Gongjindan.
We then plan your prescription and how to take it
around your current health and goals.

  1. 01We manage the herbs from the day they arrive through storage and pre-processing.
  2. 02We decoct herbal medicine and compound Gongjindan in-house.
  3. 03We adjust your prescription and how you take it to your health and goals.
