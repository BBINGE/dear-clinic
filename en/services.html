01 / BE DEER

BE DEER Weight Care

Beyond the number on the scale,
we begin with why your body is changing.

BE DEER does not rely on weight alone.
We consider metabolism and constitution, appetite and sleep,
digestion and everyday habits,
then plan an approach suited to your current condition.

  1. 01We assess metabolism and constitution together.
  2. 02We consider appetite, sleep, digestion, and daily routines.
  3. 03We plan an individualized prescription for your current condition.

02 / DEAR GONGJINDAN

Dear Gongjindan

The quality of Gongjindan
begins with the steps you cannot see.

DEAR does not use externally supplied finished Gongjindan.
Medicinal ingredients are selected and prepared in-house.
After reviewing your current condition and purpose for taking it,
we discuss the appropriate direction together.

  1. 01Director Minji Kim prepares Gongjindan in-house.
  2. 02We manage every step, from selecting and storing ingredients to preparation.
  3. 03We provide guidance on timing and use based on your health and purpose.

03 / HERBAL DECOCTION

In-house Constitutional Herbal Medicine

Prepared in-house
for each person’s current condition.

DEAR manages the receiving, storage, and pre-processing of ingredients,
the decoction of herbal medicine, and the preparation of Gongjindan in-house.
We then plan prescriptions and directions for use
around your current health and purpose.

  1. 01We manage ingredients from receiving through storage and pre-processing.
  2. 02Herbal medicine is decocted, and Gongjindan is prepared, in-house.
  3. 03We adjust prescriptions and directions for use to your health and purpose.

04 / DEER BALANCE

Deer Balance

When sleep, stress,
and the rhythm of daily life fall out of balance.

We consider insomnia, anxiety, low mood, and difficulty concentrating
alongside fatigue, tension, and digestive discomfort.
By reviewing emotional and physical changes together,
we identify a care direction suited to your current condition.

  1. 01We review changes in sleep and emotional state.
  2. 02We consider fatigue, tension, and digestive discomfort together.
  3. 03We discuss a care and daily-life plan suited to your current condition.
