BE DEER BODY COMPOSITION ARCHIVE

What a single weight figure hides,
the change you cannot see

The state at which treatment began, and the pace of change, differ from person to person. Rather than one striking result, seventeen different records are placed side by side. The figures are not overstated; the measured course is shown as it is.

About these records

These records are published with the consent of the individuals concerned, with personal details hidden. Body composition measurements can vary with hydration, meals, activity, bowel status and the conditions of measurement, and the course of care differs with each person’s condition, treatment period, lifestyle and adherence. An individual record cannot be read as a result that will appear the same for everyone.
