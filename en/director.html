WHY DEAR

Why DEAR started

Since I was young, I have wanted to do many things—and to do them well.

Over time, many wishes faded, but the desire to open a Korean medicine clinic and the question of what kind of clinic it should be only became clearer.

Moments spent typing at a monitor instead of meeting a patient’s eyes,
moments when a busy schedule left too little time to hear what brought someone in that day,
and moments when a lack of explanation left a patient feeling burdened or frustrated—

Patient's today
Treatment that cannot be heard.

At least here
I didn't want you to face it.

I did not want those moments to be repeated here.

The name DEAR embodies my philosophy of care.

The word “dear” carries a sense of someone precious and deeply valued.
I hoped DEAR could be a place where care also brings reassurance and ease.

With that in mind, DEAR Korean Medicine Clinic was started.

The name DEAR holds
the attitude I want to maintain in care.

A place where each person is treated as someone precious,
and can feel a little more at ease.

Face people first,
I will listen to the story slowly.

In an era where many things change rapidly, there is so much information that it is confusing to know what information is needed.
Nowadays, it seems like we can easily find anything through AI search.

However, spending time with someone who slowly listens to someone's story has become more precious.

I always try to meet people first and listen to their stories.

I continue to broaden my perspective so that my knowledge and experience can be useful to the people I meet.

This is because I want to be someone who can talk with patients about their lives, emotions, and daily balance.

Here, I hope you can
speak a little more comfortably
and carry a little less worry.
