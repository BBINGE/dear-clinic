Information handling by the Disoongi AI guide

Disoongi is an AI guide providing information about care areas, visits and booking channels. It does not diagnose, prescribe or confirm bookings. Ask general questions without names, contact details or medical records.

Transmission and your choices

Each message is encrypted in transit through Cloudflare Workers to Anthropic API. Request limits use a hash of the connection IP; hashing does not make it fully anonymous. The usage-limit table stores daily request totals only, not conversations or IP addresses. An access code is used only for restricted tests. Access codes, session identifiers and IP addresses are not included in the Anthropic request body. Anthropic stores data in the United States; its official notice says default processing may be routed to select countries in the US, Europe, Asia and Australia. Cloudflare uses a global network. Korea-only processing is not guaranteed.

Before chat starts, we separately confirm age 14 or older and consent for personal data, health information and overseas transfers. Phone and external appointment guidance remain available without consent. The server stores a temporary consent ID, notice version, acceptance time and expiry separately from chat content. It becomes invalid after 30 minutes. Withdrawal deletes the active record; expired records are removed on a subsequent request or scheduled cleanup. Provider recovery backups may retain copies for a period. “End chat and withdraw consent” stops further sending and clears browser conversations, but does not instantly erase data already sent to providers.

Do not enter names, telephone or national identification numbers, addresses, medical records or information identifying yourself or others. There is no complete automatic anonymization. DEAR does not store conversation content in its own server database or logs, or send it to GA4/NAVER analytics or advertising events.

Commercial Anthropic API data is not used for training by default; optional data sharing or feedback may be exceptions. Clearing browser history does not immediately delete provider data. On shared devices, end the chat and clear site data.

Stopping AI use stops further message transmission; blocking cookies alone does not. Telephone and external booking/contact channels remain available without AI, and chat content is not automatically forwarded to them. Contact the privacy officer above to exercise your rights.

Article 1. Purposes of processing

DEAR processes the minimum personal information necessary to provide medical care, verify patients and manage records, collect and refund fees, confirm insurance eligibility and submit lawful claims, manage appointments and notices, respond to inquiries and complaints, and fulfill duties under applicable laws. If a purpose changes, DEAR takes the measures required by law, including obtaining separate consent where necessary.

Article 2. Categories of personal information

Information may include name, date of birth, sex, address, contact details, identity and insurance eligibility information; resident or foreign registration numbers where processing is permitted by law; symptoms, medical history, medications, test results, diagnoses, prescriptions, treatment and progress; payment, insurance and certificate information; and guardian details for minors or patients requiring a representative.

Website use

This website has no membership or patient-information booking form. The Disoongi AI guide processes typed messages as described in AI information handling below. Cookies and online identifiers, pages viewed, access times, referral sources and Naver Search Ads (Powerlink) inflow parameters (including NaPm), device and browser information, approximate region, and clicks on call or booking buttons may be processed automatically. A click records only an attempt to open external booking or initiate a call; this tracking does not receive booking-completion status, call-connection status, or consultation content. After moving to an external service, that service provider's privacy policy applies.

Article 3. Retention period

Information is retained for the period required by law or necessary for its purpose. Under Korea's Medical Service Act Enforcement Rule, key periods include: patient register 5 years; medical records 10 years; prescriptions 2 years; surgery records 10 years; examination findings 5 years; radiographs and reports 5 years; nursing and midwifery records 5 years; and copies of medical certificates 3 years. A period applies only when the relevant record is created and may be extended where permitted for continuing care.

Article 4. Provision to third parties

DEAR does not provide personal information to third parties beyond the stated purposes unless the patient consents or the law specifically permits or requires it. Lawful recipients may include the National Health Insurance Service, Health Insurance Review and Assessment Service, competent government bodies, courts, and investigative agencies acting under a lawful request.

Article 5. Entrustment of processing

DEAR entrusts Google LLC with website usage statistics and key-button analysis through Google Analytics 4(GA4). DEAR entrusts NAVER Corporation with visit, referral and page-usage statistics through NAVER Analytics, and with website inflow and booking/call-button performance analysis through Naver Search Ads (Powerlink) conversion tracking. Where processing is entrusted, DEAR documents purpose limitations, safeguards, subcontracting limits, supervision, and responsibility as required by Article 26 of the Personal Information Protection Act and discloses material changes.

Article 6. Overseas processing

When Google Analytics 4(GA4) is used, cookie identifiers, page visits, access times, referral sources, device and browser information, approximate region, and key-button events may be transmitted over an encrypted network to Google LLC and processed in the United States and other countries where Google operates data centers. The purpose is website analytics and service improvement, and retention is governed by the Analytics setting for up to 14 months.

DEAR does not send names, contact details, medical records, or other directly identifying patient information in analytics events. You may block or delete cookies in your browser to refuse this processing without losing ordinary access to the website.

Article 7. Destruction

When a retention period expires or a purpose is achieved, information is destroyed without delay unless continued retention is legally required. Electronic files are deleted so they cannot be restored; paper is shredded or incinerated; and storage media is securely erased or physically destroyed.

Article 8. Rights of data subjects and representatives

You may request access, correction or deletion, suspension of processing, withdrawal of consent, or an explanation of or objection to an automated decision. A legal representative may exercise rights for a child under 14. Requests may be made in person or by calling DEAR, and identity or authority may be verified. Rights may be limited where another law requires retention, another person's rights could be harmed, or the law restricts access or suspension.

Article 9. Security measures

DEAR applies administrative, technical, and physical safeguards, including staff oversight and training, minimum access privileges, access-control and security software, protection of credentials and important information, preservation of access logs, and controlled storage and destruction of documents and media.

Article 10. Cookies and analytics

DEAR uses Google Analytics 4(GA4), NAVER Analytics, and Naver Search Ads (Powerlink) conversion tracking to understand website use, improve services, and measure website inflow and conversions from Powerlink. Cookies and online identifiers, including the `_ga` cookie, may distinguish visitors and sessions. Page visits, access times, referral sources and Naver Search Ads (Powerlink) inflow parameters (including NaPm), IP address, device, browser, operating-system and screen information, approximate region, and clicks on key call or booking buttons may be collected.

A conversion records only an attempt to open external booking or initiate a call, not a completed booking, connected call, or consultation content. This information is not used to create profiles for personalized advertising, and DEAR does not enter or transmit names, contact details, medical records, or other directly identifying patient information in analytics events. You may reject or delete cookies in your browser without losing ordinary access to the website or its call and external-booking links.

Article 11. Automated decisions

DEAR does not use personal information to make fully automated decisions about eligibility for care, treatment methods, or other matters that significantly affect patient rights or obligations.
