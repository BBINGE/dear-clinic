ALWAYS “DEAR” YOU

Care that begins by listening to you.

At DEAR, we look beyond a single symptom and consider how your body, daily life, sleep, and emotions are connected.

ABOUT DEAR

Your symptoms are only one part of your story.

Each person experiences discomfort for different reasons. We take time to listen, explain in clear language, and look for a direction that can fit into your present life without adding unnecessary burden.

01

We consider the body and daily life together.

We review your current condition, routines, sleep, stress, and other factors that may be connected.

02

We listen and explain carefully.

We do not rush your story. We explain the assessment and possible options in words you can understand.

03

We seek a manageable approach.

Rather than asking you to simply endure, we discuss a plan you may be able to continue in everyday life.

04

We think about life after the visit.

Our goal is to help the care you receive connect meaningfully with your daily routine.

FIVE AREAS OF CARE

We begin with how you feel today.

You do not need to describe every symptom perfectly. Tell us what has changed and what feels difficult.

FOCUS

When focus is difficult

For ongoing fatigue, brain fog, reduced concentration, or difficulty maintaining a clear rhythm during study and work.

CALM

When the mind cannot rest

For persistent tension, anxiety, sensitivity, or difficulty falling asleep and resting well.

RESTORE

When you need to replenish

For lingering fatigue, low energy, burnout, and a sense that recovery is slower than before.

RELIEF

When something feels uncomfortable

For pain, headache, digestive discomfort, constipation, or other signals your body is currently sending.

SHAPE

When beginning a healthy change

We consider metabolism, swelling, appetite, sleep, digestion, activity, and habits—not only body weight or shape.

DEAR SERVICES

Four suggestions for your present condition and life.

01

Be Deer Weight Management

Personalized care that considers metabolism, constitution, appetite, sleep, digestion, and lifestyle patterns together.

02

DEAR Gongjindan

Ingredients are selected and preparation is managed in the clinic after reviewing your current health and purpose of use.

03

In-clinic Herbal Decoction

Herbal prescriptions are prepared and decocted in the clinic according to your condition, constitution, and intended use.

04

Deer Balance

Personalized care that considers interconnected signals such as stress, fatigue, sleep, and daily rhythm.

A consultation with a licensed Korean medicine doctor is required to determine whether a particular treatment is appropriate.
